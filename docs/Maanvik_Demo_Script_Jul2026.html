Act 2 — Killer questions (the heart of the pitch)

3:00

Question 1: “If your IndiaMART subscription stops tomorrow — can you still call everyone who enquired last year?”
PAUSE 4 seconds. Do not answer. Look at BR, then ASR, then guest. Let the silence work.
“On IndiaMART, that list lives in their app. On your own site — it’s in your Gmail and your customer tracking. That’s the whole difference in one sentence.”
Question 2: “Someone enquires at 11 at night. Tomorrow morning — is it in your Gmail, or only if you open IndiaMART’s app?”
PAUSE 3 seconds.
Question 3: “When a college principal or architect visits your page — do they see only you, or five competitors beside you?”
PAUSE 3 seconds.
“Mall versus your own showroom — IndiaMART is the mall. What I built for Maanvik is the showroom. Let me show you.”

3g · Behind the scenes (2 min) — no live login needed

Objection handling — short answers

“We already pay IndiaMART.” — “Good — keep it for discovery. This is your showroom, not a replacement.”
“₹43k / ₹48k is a lot.” — “One institutional order typically covers it. IndiaMART listing is similar money every year — this you own.”
“Who maintains email and forms after go-live?” — “Optional Digital Operations — quarterly checklist on forms, billing@, renewals. List ₹2k/month; Maanvik founding ₹1,500. Cheaper than hiring someone.”
“Can you do social media too?” — “Yes — that’s a separate quote after the website is live. I don’t bundle it so you’re not paying for what you don’t need yet.”
“ASR: why not switch now?” — “You’ve paid Maximiser — use it. We add alongside; renewal is the decision point.”
“Guest: what will it cost me?” — “Discovery first. Foundation from ₹48,000 for new clients — Maanvik was ₹43,000. Depends on modules.”

Do not say

· “You wasted money on Maximiser” · “Drop IndiaMART” (to ASR) · “ASR should have called me first”
· “Lead ownership” without explaining email · MDC / CRM / GA4 without plain English first
· Promise marketing in base package · Push guest to sign today

Power phrases to repeat

  • “Being found” vs “being chosen”
  • “Mall vs showroom”
  • “Your email and WhatsApp” — not “lead ownership”
  • “Only what you need from day one”
  • “Pause” after killer questions — your strongest tool
