This agreement is between Sravan Kumar Sindhe (“Provider”) and the client named above (“Client”) for outsourced digital operations on the Client’s owned website and business email — not website redesign, content, or marketing unless quoted separately.

1 · Scope of services

Provider will perform the services for the selected tier as described in Digital_Operations_Retainer.html and the quarterly run sheet Quarterly_Ops_Checklist.html, including as applicable:

  • Maintain agreed email aliases and forwarding rules (orders@, billing@, refunds@, accounts@, support@ — up to tier limit)
  • Quarterly enquiry pipeline test (website forms → orders@ inbox → HubSpot where configured)
  • Renewal reminders for domain and Google Workspace (60 / 30 / 7 days before expiry)
  • Support tickets within monthly cap (30 minutes each unless otherwise agreed)
  • Additional tier items: monthly enquiry snapshot (Growth), catalogue updates (Growth), Razorpay checks (Commerce)

2 · Not included

  • Replying to buyer or customer emails on Client’s behalf
  • Managing IndiaMART or other marketplace catalogue content
  • 24/7 or on-call support
  • Product photography, social media, advertising, SEO, or copywriting
  • Unlimited website changes or new feature development (quoted separately at catalogue rates)
  • Google Workspace, domain registration, AWS hosting, Razorpay fees — paid directly by Client

3 · Client responsibilities

  • Keep Google Workspace and domain registrations current and paid
  • Provide timely access to DNS, Workspace admin, Formspree, HubSpot, and hosting when Provider needs to perform checks
  • Respond to buyer enquiries and orders — Provider monitors infrastructure, not sales inbox content
  • Notify Provider of staff or email routing changes within 7 days

4 · Fees & payment

  • Monthly retainer fee as stated above, invoiced monthly in advance or arrears as agreed
  • GST @ 18% added if Provider is GST-registered at time of invoice
  • Payment due within        days of invoice (suggest: 7 days)
  • Work beyond included tickets or tier scope billed at à la carte rates in Service_Price_Catalogue.html with Client approval before work starts

5 · Term & cancellation

  • Initial term: 12 months from start date unless otherwise written above
  • Minimum commitment: 3 months — early cancellation within first 3 months: Client pays remaining months of minimum term or one month fee, whichever is lower (adjust if you prefer simpler: “pay through end of month”)
  • After minimum term: either party may cancel with 30 days’ written notice (email acceptable)
  • At cancellation: Provider delivers final email matrix export; no refund of current month unless services not performed

6 · Response times

ItemTarget
Support ticket (Essential / Growth)2 business days
Payment pipeline issue (Commerce)48 hours
Quarterly checklistWithin 5 business days of quarter end
Emergency (site down, forms completely broken)Best effort same business day — not guaranteed 24/7

7 · Confidentiality & data

Provider may access Client business email routing, analytics, and CRM for operational purposes only. Client data is not sold or shared with third parties except subprocessors already in use (Google, Formspree, HubSpot, AWS) as part of the existing stack.

8 · Limitation

Provider is not liable for lost revenue from missed enquiries caused by third-party outages (Google, Formspree, AWS, IndiaMART), Client failure to renew domain or Workspace, or Client delay in approving fixes. Liability capped at fees paid in the 3 months preceding the claim.

9 · Entire agreement

This document, the tier description in Digital_Operations_Retainer.html, and the signed email allocation matrix constitute the agreement. Website build work, if any, is under a separate quote. Changes in writing by both parties.
