Digital Operations Retainer

Sravan Kumar Sindhe · Outsourced digital office ops for SMEs · v1.0 · July 2026 · Prices excl. GST unless stated
What this is: Not “website maintenance” or a developer on call. Outsourced digital operations — professional email addresses, working enquiry forms, renewal reminders, and a quarterly health check. You focus on orders; I keep the pipeline running.
Who it’s for: Owners who cannot hire IT staff but need billing@, orders@, and forms to work without silent failures.
Starts when: Website go-live (or Phase 1 email setup for Path B). Optional — not bundled into Foundation build fee.
Killer question: If a buyer emails billing@yourdomain.com and it bounces — do you find out, or do they just go quiet?
